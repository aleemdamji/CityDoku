Who we are

Citydoku is a daily city trivia game made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [CITY], Canada. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What you can do without an account

You can play every grid without signing in. Your progress, streak and settings are then stored only on your device.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Sign-in details: your email address and, if you use Apple or Google, a sign-in identifier from that provider. Apple’s “Hide My Email” is supported.
  • Game data: the username you choose, your answers and guesses for each grid, scores, streaks, and the friend leagues you create or join.
  • Feedback: anything you send through “Send feedback or a correction”, with the grid it was about.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and we don’t sell personal information.

What other players can see

Your username, your scores and your answers to past grids are visible to other players, because leaderboards and rarity scores are calculated from everyone’s answers. Your email address is never shown.

Reminders

Daily reminders in the iOS app are local notifications scheduled on your device. On the web, the calendar reminder is a file you add to your own calendar. We don’t send marketing email.

Where data is stored

Deleting your data

Use “Delete account” in the game while signed in. This permanently removes your account, username, scores and league memberships. You can also email [CONTACT EMAIL].

Children

Citydoku is a general-audience game. Accounts are for people 13 and older.
